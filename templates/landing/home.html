Accessing the Current Phase

Pragati is currently deployed under a managed access protocol for accredited departments and participating academic teams. To evaluate or participate in this phase:

1
Submit an Onboarding Request: Fill in your institution, department, and evaluation scope.
2
Administrative Review: The system administrator verifies the department details and issues a license key.
3
Activate & Sign In: Enter the issued key on the activation page to unlock the workspace for your session.
