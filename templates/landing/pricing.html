Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Pragati's institutional access and plans.

Can we evaluate Pragati with a single department first?

Yes! Choose the Free Department Pilot to test marks entry, attendance, and NBA matrix calculation with up to 50 faculty seats at zero cost.

How do activation keys work?

Once your request is approved by the KalpKrafts team, you receive a unique key (e.g. PRG-XXXX) to unlock instant platform access for your campus.

Where is institutional student data hosted?

All institutional data is strictly hosted within sovereign Indian cloud infrastructure with automated daily backups and AES-256 encryption at rest.

Yes, we support formal institutional Net 30 purchase orders, GST invoices, wire transfers, and campus-wide enterprise licensing agreements.
